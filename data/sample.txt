Company Leave Policy

1. Annual Leave
Employees receive 20 days of annual leave per year.

2. Applying for Leave
Employees must submit leave requests through the
company leave management system.

3. Leave Approval
The employee's manager reviews the request.
The manager can approve or reject the request
based on staffing requirements.

4. Emergency Leave
Employees who need emergency leave must notify
their manager as soon as possible.

5. Sick Leave
Employees must inform their manager when they
cannot attend work due to illness.

6. HR Department
The HR department maintains employee leave records
and answers questions about the leave policy.
